Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Short version: Your files never leave your device. We process everything in your browser. We collect no files, no images, no videos — ever.

1. What We Collect

ClearSend collects only the minimum information needed to operate the service:

Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We use this only to identify your account and manage your subscription.

Usage data: We store a daily count of how many files you have processed, linked to a browser fingerprint. This count resets every 24 hours. No file content is ever stored.

Payment information: Payments are processed by Flutterwave. We never see or store your card number, mobile money PIN, or banking details.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

— Your photos, images, or videos
— The content of any file you optimize
— Your WhatsApp contacts or messages
— Your location
— Any data from your camera or microphone

3. How File Processing Works

All file optimization happens entirely inside your web browser using WebAssembly technology (ffmpeg.wasm). Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party server. The moment you close or refresh the page, all processed files are permanently deleted from memory.

4. Browser Storage

We use IndexedDB (local browser storage) to remember your daily usage count and login session. This data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

5. Third-Party Services

Google Fonts: Used for typography. Google may log your IP address when fonts are loaded.

Flutterwave: Used for payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment transactions.

Vercel: Our hosting provider. They may log standard web server data (IP address, browser type, pages visited).

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Since we store no file content, there is no file data to delete.

7. Children

ClearSend is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@clearsend.app
